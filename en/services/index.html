Services

My services as a project lead

My main work is running AI and digital projects end to end as a project lead; the technical areas below sit under that management. Each service page covers what I do, the steps, the deliverables, the tools I use and how I manage that area.

Areas

  • AI Projects — AI agents, LLM RAG, fine-tuning, ISO/IEC 42001 governance
  • Project Management — from discovery to maintenance, with scope, budget and risk control
  • IT Consulting and Team Leadership
  • Web Development — 200+ corporate websites
  • Mobile App Development — 10+ apps
  • Backend and System Architecture
  • DevOps and Infrastructure
  • Information Security and ISO Compliance Projects

All services follow the same principle: clarify the need, think about security and governance from the start, and leave a sustainable structure after delivery. From a small corporate presentation site to a multi-layer AI integration, we set the scope together. I work remotely; my experience with teams across time zones is on the FAQ page.

How do I proceed?

  • In a free intro call we clarify the need and the goal together.
  • Scope, schedule and deliverables are written down; ambiguous items are discussed up front.
  • Development moves in stages and you see a working result at each one.
  • After launch, support is planned for maintenance, updates and security.

None of my services is an off-the-shelf package; technology and method are chosen for each project. For references and past work, just write to me directly: WhatsApp (+90 531 679 10 14).
